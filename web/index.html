What influenced each hourly score?

What the score means: the selected model estimates how much this hourly record resembles the positive label in the PhysioNet/CinC 2019 training data. That label begins six hours before the recorded sepsis onset and remains positive afterward. Scores from the three models are not calibrated to be directly comparable, and none is a patient's medical probability.

An active rule means the demonstration cutoff was crossed under the displayed 1-of-5 policy. It is a model output only, not a clinical alert or recommendation.

Exploratory retrospective evaluation: the rule covered 81.4% of sepsis warning windows; 24.9% of patients without a sepsis label received at least one alert. The project team had already examined this test split, so these are not independent final performance estimates.
